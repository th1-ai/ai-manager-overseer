# Guest FAQ - The Marlow House (fixture data)

Sample data for `make demo` and the tests.

## What time can I check in?

From 15:00. Bags can be held at reception if you arrive earlier.

## Is breakfast included?

Depends on the rate booked. If not, EUR 20 per person, served 07:00 to 10:00.

## Can I cancel my booking?

Free up to 48 hours before arrival. Inside 48 hours we charge the first
night.

## Do you take data requests seriously?

Yes. Any request to see, correct or delete personal data is worked by
Compliance / GDPR AI against a 30-day statutory clock - see
`docs/sub-agents.md`.
